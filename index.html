02 — The atelier

Shaped slowly, by hand, to outlast the season.

Aurelle began with a single bench and a simple belief: jewelry should be quiet. No logos, no noise, only form, weight and the way light settles on metal.

Each piece is drawn, cast and finished by hand. Gold pieces are made to order, silver pieces are made in small batches, and every one is hallmarked.

Honest metals18K gold and sterling silver 925, ethically sourced and hallmarked.
Made with timeGold pieces in 3–4 weeks, silver pieces ready to ship in 7–10 days.

03 — Questions

Good to know

How long does a piece take?

Silver pieces ship within 7–10 days. Gold pieces are made to order and take 3–4 weeks. We confirm the date before you commit.

Can I see a piece before buying?

Yes. Book a private viewing at the atelier, or ask for a video appointment with the pieces in hand.

Can you resize a ring?

Yes. Resizing is free in the first 90 days. Tell us your size or visit us to be measured.

What is your return policy?

Unworn pieces can be returned within 30 days. Made-to-order gold pieces can be exchanged or resized, but not refunded.

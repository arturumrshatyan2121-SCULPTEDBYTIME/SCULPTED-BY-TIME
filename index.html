Specifications

The details.

Nutrition

≈ 650 kcal for the Signature, 38 g protein. Full table on request at the counter.

Allergens

Gluten, dairy, sesame, mustard. Made in a kitchen that handles nuts.

Sourcing

Local beef ground in-house each morning; buns from a bakery two streets away.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Yes, for groups of ten or more we also make a plant-based stack, with a grilled mushroom instead of the patty.

Can I change the ingredients?

Of course. Tell us what to add or leave out in the notes when you order.

Do you cater events?

Yes, for groups of ten or more. Write to us in the form below and we'll reply within a day.

How fast is delivery?

20–30 minutes inside the city ring. Pickup is usually ready in 8.

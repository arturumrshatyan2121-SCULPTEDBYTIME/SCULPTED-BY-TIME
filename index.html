(02) — Our story

Shaped slowly, by hand, to outlast the season.

Aurelle began as a single bench and a belief that jewelry should be quiet. No logos, no noise: only form, weight and the way light settles on metal.

Each piece is drawn, cast and finished in our atelier and made to order, so the one you receive is the only one of its kind.

Honest metals18K white gold and sterling silver, ethically sourced and hallmarked.
Made to orderCast, set and polished by hand. Most pieces are ready in three to six weeks.

(03) — Questions

Good to know

How long does a piece take?

Most pieces are made to order in three to six weeks. We confirm the date before you commit.

Can I see a piece before buying?

Yes. Book a private viewing at the atelier, or ask for a video appointment with the pieces in hand.

Can you resize or customise?

Most designs can be resized or adapted in metal, stone and finish. Tell us what you have in mind.

What if I change my mind?

Unworn pieces can be returned within 30 days. Custom commissions are made just for you and are final.
